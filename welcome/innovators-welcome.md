## Our Living Roadmap

We are not following a fixed, predetermined path. We are exploring the most important challenges together. Our roadmap is a **living document**, evolving with every contribution.

### Our Current Exploration Zones

1. **The Verification Puzzle**

   - How do we verify real-world impact before we have a full planetary sensor network?  
   - We are starting with community and expert verification, building towards a future of automated, triangulated truth.

2. **The Governance Engine**

   - How do we make collective decisions that are both wise and truly decentralized?  
   - Our compass points to Proof-of-Goodwill, where your voice in governance is earned by your verifiable contributions.

3. **The Abundance Flywheel**

   - How do we create an economy that generates more value than it consumes?  
   - We are designing a system where Universal Basic Income, Impact Certificates, and regenerative investments create a virtuous cycle of prosperity.

## The Innovation Flow

We operate on a simple, powerful cycle: **Brief → Build → Recognize**.

- **We Set the Compass**  
  We provide clear briefs on **what** we are building and **why**. Each brief outlines the challenge, its importance, and what success looks like.

- **You Chart the Course**  
  The community figures out **how** to build it. We welcome multiple approaches, creative solutions, and questions that challenge our assumptions.

- **We Celebrate Contributions**  
  Every meaningful development is recognized and valued, forming the foundation of our shared progress.
